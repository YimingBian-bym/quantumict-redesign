## Biography

Hao Wu is a direct-entry doctoral student studying ab initio methods for strongly correlated electronic systems. His research focuses on combining neural-network quantum states with auxiliary-field quantum Monte Carlo, with particular attention to how many-body trial wave functions control projection accuracy in challenging transition-metal and f-electron systems.

## Research Interests

- Strongly correlated electronic structure
- Auxiliary-field quantum Monte Carlo
- Neural-network quantum states
- Transition-metal clusters and magnetic properties

## Current Research

His current work develops neural-network-quantum-state-enhanced AFQMC methods. He studies how the representation of many-body trial wave functions affects the accuracy of the phaseless approximation and applies these methods to the electronic structure and magnetic properties of strongly correlated systems such as multinuclear transition-metal clusters.

## Education

- **B.S. in Applied Physics**, University of Science and Technology of China
