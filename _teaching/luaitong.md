## Biography

Aitong Lu is a doctoral student whose research focuses on quantum machine learning and AI-assisted quantum computing. Her work explores the intersection of machine learning, quantum algorithms, and scientific computing, with particular emphasis on neural-network quantum states and quantum–classical hybrid methods for complex quantum many-body problems.

## Research Interests

- Quantum machine learning
- AI-assisted quantum computing
- Neural-network quantum states and Transformer models
- Quantum–classical hybrid algorithms

## Current Research

Her current research studies many-body wave-function representation and optimization using neural-network quantum states, Transformer architectures, and quantum sampling. The goal is to develop AI–quantum hybrid methods for challenging electronic-structure problems and improve the accuracy and efficiency of calculations for strongly correlated systems.
