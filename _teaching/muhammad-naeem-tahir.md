## Biography

Muhammad Naeem Tahir is a Research Associate at the University of Science and Technology of China. His research focuses on accurate and efficient first-principles electronic-structure methods and their implementation in the FHI-aims code. He specializes in density functional theory, the random-phase approximation, and related beyond-DFT methods for reliable calculations of energies and forces in molecular and condensed-phase systems.

## Research Interests

- Density functional theory
- Random-phase approximation and related methods
- Analytical gradients, response theory, energies, and forces
- Efficient algorithms for large-scale electronic-structure calculations

## Current Research

His current research develops analytical gradients, response-theory formulations, and efficient algorithms for RPA and related beyond-DFT methods. A central goal is to enable accurate structural optimization and force calculations for periodic systems, extending high-level electronic-structure methods to computational materials science and chemistry.

## Education and Research Training
