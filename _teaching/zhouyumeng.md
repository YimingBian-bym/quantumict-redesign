## Biography

Yumeng Zhou is a doctoral student working on neural-network quantum states and high-performance computing. Her research spans artificial intelligence, quantum chemistry, and scientific computing, with an emphasis on applying deep learning and large-scale parallel computing to quantum many-body problems.

## Research Interests

- Neural-network quantum states
- High-performance scientific computing
- Classical simulation of quantum circuits
- Quantum-state sampling and selected configuration interaction

## Current Research

Her current research develops high-performance methods for quantum simulators. She studies classical quantum-circuit simulation, quantum-state sampling, and parallel acceleration for quantum-chemistry ground-state calculations. By combining neural-network quantum states with selected configuration-interaction methods, she aims to identify and reconstruct important configurations while reducing computational and storage complexity.
