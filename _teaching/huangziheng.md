## Biography

Ziheng Huang is a master's student interested in quantum mechanics, quantum simulation, and quantum computing. His academic background is in applied physics, and his current work explores algorithmic approaches to quantum-chemistry simulation.

## Research Interests

- Quantum simulation
- Quantum computing
- Quantum algorithms for chemistry

## Current Research

His current research applies Trotter-based algorithms to concrete quantum-chemistry systems, with the long-term goal of evaluating routes toward practical quantum advantage in chemically relevant simulations.

## Education

- **B.S. in Applied Physics**, Hainan University, 2022–2026
