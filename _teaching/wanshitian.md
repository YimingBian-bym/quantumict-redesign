## Biography

Shitian Wan is a master's student working on first-principles methods and their applications to condensed-matter systems. His research uses density functional theory and density-functional perturbation theory to study electronic structure, lattice dynamics, and dielectric properties. He is also interested in machine-learning approaches for force fields, band-structure prediction, and high-throughput materials screening.

## Research Interests

- Density functional theory and density-functional perturbation theory
- First-principles calculations of lattice dynamics and dielectric properties
- Infrared optical response
- Machine learning for materials-property prediction

## Current Research

His current work investigates phonon spectra and dielectric response in crystalline materials. He is studying infrared optical properties, implementing Born effective-charge calculations with DFPT in FHI-aims, and constructing first-principles datasets to evaluate graph neural networks for direct prediction of infrared dielectric functions.

## Education

- **M.S. in Physics**, University of Science and Technology of China, 2025–present
- **B.S. in Applied Physics**, University of Science and Technology of China, 2021–2025
