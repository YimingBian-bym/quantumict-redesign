Our group develops and applies high-performance computational methods to accelerate scientific discovery.

## High-Performance Computing

We explore efficient computational approaches for large-scale scientific simulations and data-intensive research.

## First-Principles Method Development

We develop and implement first-principles methods for understanding materials and physical processes at the atomic scale.

## Kinetic Monte Carlo Methods

We develop and apply kinetic Monte Carlo methods to study long-time-scale dynamical processes.
