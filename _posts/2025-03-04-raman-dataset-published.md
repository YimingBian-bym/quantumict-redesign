<p class="news-article__meta">Publication · 4 March 2025</p>

<p>
A high-throughput study of Raman spectra for two-dimensional materials has been
published in <em>Scientific Data</em>.
</p>

<p>
The work presents an automated computational workflow based on all-electron
density-functional perturbation theory. Using this workflow, the researchers
calculated Raman spectra for <strong>3,504 two-dimensional materials</strong>
and organized the results as an accessible data resource.
</p>

<p>
The dataset provides geometry information, Raman frequencies, and intensities,
and is intended to support materials characterization, theoretical studies, and
future machine-learning applications.
</p>
